# Brag Plan: Transguard Workforce Intelligence

## What is this app?
A night-shift desk for Transguard's guarding business: it shows tonight's open posts and the guards likely not to show, fills every gap with an optimiser in milliseconds, answers guards in their own language, and triages facilities fault reports.

## The angle
The night supervisor's problem, solved on screen. It opens with the count every supervisor dreads: 702 posts, 4 empty, 3 guards who may not show. Then the product closes the gaps live: one click, "7 gaps filled by the optimiser in 9 ms", and the headline flips to "Every post is covered tonight". Two quick proofs that it covers the whole workforce, not just rosters: a guard asks in Urdu and gets his shift back in Urdu, and a stuck hospital lift becomes a P1 with a technician assigned. Calm, confident, operational; the numbers do the talking.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Charcoal night ground. "702 posts tonight." settles, then "4 are empty." lands in Transguard red on the first strong hit, then "3 guards may not show." in crest gold. A count the owner recognises before any product appears.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The real Tonight screen: headline "4 posts open and 3 guards at risk tonight", the map, the Needs action list. The cursor clicks **Fill all gaps**.
- The plan sheet slides in from the right, as in the app: seven assignments, "7 gaps filled by the optimiser in 9 ms", AED 0 overtime.
- The payoff: "Every post is covered tonight", 100.0%, 702 of 702.
- Worker assistant: Asif Khan's phone, question and answer both in Urdu.
- FM tickets: Deira Hospital lift, P1, "Respond within 1 hour", Bishnu Rai assigned.

## Outro / punchline
Transguard crest, "Workforce Intelligence", then "Concept demo for Transguard Group. Simulated sites and guards." and "Built by NinjaTech". Held 2+ s.

## User flow worth showing
Tonight (see the gaps) → Fill all gaps (plan in milliseconds) → Approve (every post covered). Then the two supporting flows: guard asks in Urdu → answer from his roster; fault report → triage → technician.

## Privacy
All guards, sites, rosters and tickets on screen are simulated (fictional names) and the app says so; no real personal data appears. The real Transguard brand appears next to simulated figures, so keep the video internal until Transguard agrees to its use.
